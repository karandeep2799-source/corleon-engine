# Writing Quality Review Rubric

- Kill common AI tells, repetitive rhetorical patterns, overused em dashes, and generic openings.
- Prefer plain, concrete English over vague, corporate, or inflated wording.
- Front-load the point in sentences, paragraphs, and sections.
- Prefer specific examples and evidence over abstract claims.
- Remove unnecessary hedges, filler, and repetition.
- Match the supplied voice and audience rather than imposing a generic style.
- Preserve factual meaning. Flag unsupported statistics, quotations, guarantees, and claims.
- Keep paragraphs short and structure easy to scan.
- For long-form web content, check title, headings, readability, useful links, and a clear close.
